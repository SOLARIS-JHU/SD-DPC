# Double Integrator

Discrete two-dimensional dynamics identification and obstacle-avoiding sparse policy learning. `configs/policy.yaml` contains the sparse-policy, NN-DPC, and NN-Distilled settings. The pretrained checkpoints are in `checkpoints/`. Use `notebooks/00_colab_workflow.ipynb` for the combined Colab tutorial or `01_system_id.ipynb` through `04_plot_evaluation_results.ipynb` for separate stages; matching commands, including `train_nn_distilled.py`, are under `scripts/DoubleIntegrator/`. The policy training support is $x_0\in[2,4]\times[-4,-2]$ and $r\in[-4,-2]\times[2,4]$; substantially different rollout points test extrapolation and may produce poor trajectories.
